Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about using LawMitra AI.

Is this formal legal representation?

No. LawMitra AI is an informational legal guidance assistant designed to educate you on applicable laws and draft preliminary communications. For representation in court, always consult a licensed advocate.

Is my issue information kept private?

Yes. All conversation history and data stay strictly in your local browser session.

Can I use this for court cases or urgent emergencies?

For immediate physical danger, always call Police Helpline 112. For online financial fraud, immediately call 1930. LawMitra assists with legal education, rights discovery, and complaint drafts.
